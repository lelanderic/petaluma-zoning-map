About this map

An unofficial, clickable reading aid for the City of Petaluma's proposed citywide zoning map (Exhibit C, draft dated November 9, 2026). It is not a City product and the zoning it shows has not been adopted. For any decision about a property, confirm with the City of Petaluma Planning Division.

Where the information comes from

  • Zones are read from the colors of the Exhibit C map image, not from parcel records.
  • Zone descriptions and standards are short paraphrases of Exhibit B (Amendments in Clean, November 9, 2026). Footnotes and exceptions in the tables are left out.
  • General Plan land use, street names and PUD/PCD names come from the City's public GIS layers, downloaded November 10, 2026.

Known limits

  • Along zone edges and on very small parcels the zone shown can be off by a few map pixels (one pixel is about 12 feet). Away from edges, zones agree with the City's parcel-level General Plan layer on over 99% of land.
  • Pointing at a street shows the nearest zone within about 70 feet. Streets themselves are not zoned on this map.
  • Overlay areas (flood plain, historic districts, senior mobile home parks, fairgrounds, T6-Open) are traced from the Exhibit C overlay page and are approximate. Four senior mobile home park areas were traced; the ordinance refers to five.
  • PUD and PCD names come from the City's current zoning layer (2008 ordinance). They cover about 95% of the proposed PUD and PCD land and may be out of date where districts have changed.
  • The map legend labels floodway inside the Central Petaluma Specific Plan as CS-FW. The ordinance text says that land is zoned P-FW.
  • The ordinance establishes two zones, R-RR and MU-TC, that do not appear in the map legend.
  • For Central Petaluma Specific Plan zones, building standards live in the SmartCode and are referenced here, not summarized.
  • The "7 simple groups" view is this map's own grouping, not a classification in the ordinance.
  • "Share of map" figures are estimates from pixel counts and leave out streets.
  • Street labels and the scale bar are placed by fitting the City's street layer to the map image, so positions are approximate.
